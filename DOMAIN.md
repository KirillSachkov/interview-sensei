# Interview preparation

**Candidate**: The person preparing for a job interview with the agent.

**Preparation goal**: An outcome the candidate explicitly confirms they want to prepare for, such as interviewing for a role across several companies or preparing for a particular interview. A candidate can revise a goal over time and pursue several goals at once.

**Interview scenario**: The stages and topics selected for a mock interview in light of a preparation goal. A scenario can start from a typical interview for the role and change as the candidate and agent learn more about the target interview.

**Practice attempt**: One occasion on which a candidate answers a question or works on a task, including what they did independently and where they used help.

**Skill**: An ability a candidate can demonstrate in practice. The same skill can be relevant to several preparation goals.

**Progress**: How a candidate’s demonstrated skills change over time. Each practice attempt belongs to the candidate’s history and counts toward every goal for which the demonstrated skill is relevant, including goals added later.

**Readiness**: A current assessment of what a candidate can demonstrate in a topic or interview stage relevant to a preparation goal, based on the available evidence.

**Mock interview**: A rehearsal of one or more interview stages, followed by feedback on the candidate’s performance. A full mock interview can be the candidate’s first practice activity and provide the initial evidence of readiness.
